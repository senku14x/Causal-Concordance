# Independent audit: experiments 007 and 008

Date: 2026-10-06. Scope: Qwen3.6-27B, brew; independent read-only analysis. Repository was being updated by Claude during onboarding. This assessment uses the canonical 007/008 reports after their report-31 corrections. No project files, outputs, configuration, dependencies, or workflows were changed. Numerical diagnostics below were computed by standalone Python from saved artifacts, without importing project modules.

## Main conclusion

007 establishes an Oracle-specific dependence on its dense 1,024-dimensional J-derived subspace at the brew colon, with the strongest registered result at L40. 008 establishes that a sparse k=25 J approximation of class-mean colour directions is a substantially weaker natural-coordinate intervention than the corresponding remainder on 82 gated development pairs from 33 tables. Neither result establishes that the model naturally computes the intermediate outside every meaningful J representation, or that the Oracle reads information the model does not use.

The old comparison between 007's dense reader decomposition and 008's sparse causal decomposition was invalid as a matched-object dissociation. Canonical reports now explicitly correct it. The replacement comparison, 007 Stage 1a/test against Stage 2, uses the same projector but still requires qualification: Stage 2 establishes approximate baseline agreement at L42, whereas the Oracle is on-contract at L40 and L44, where the model-side result is explicitly **mixed**, with positive J advantages against random controls. “No special purchase beyond content share at L40–44” still overstates the evidence.

There are two additional implementation-dependent interpretation issues: 008's complementary clamp holds coefficients in a **joint** dictionary fixed, rather than holding isolated J projections fixed; and its nominal norm-matched arms match clean-reference edits, not the actual sequence of writes along perturbed trajectories. These are well-defined interventions, not evidence of arbitrary numerical failure. Their interpretation must match their actual definition.

## Paper-to-project correspondence

I read the local Workspace HTML extraction and the three substantive note documents in the requested order. The paper's strongest relevant result is §3.3/Fig.16: independently obtained intermediate vectors, sparse nonnegative k=25 decomposition, swaps of components, and complementary clamps. It reports 61% top-one target-answer flips for J, 28% for the remainder, and 6% for the remainder with relevant J coordinates clamped. Its tested prompts and Claude models differ from Qwen brew. Its interventions cover every token position over a layer band. Its own §3.5 demonstrates readable content without causal involvement in some tasks, so mere reader-use mismatch is not a contradiction of its complete argument.

Three objects remain separate:

1. Token-readout J-lens vectors.
2. Sparse k=25 pursuit reconstruction of a particular class-mean direction, using folded unembedding atoms in 008.
3. Dense top-1,024 eigenspace of unit-normalized raw-unembedding J vectors in 007.

Sparse residuals are not globally “outside J-space”: the dictionary may span the whole residual stream. They are remainders after a particular limited-sparsity fit. NNLS makes the remainder orthogonal to the *active support of its own fit*, not to every J vector, the other colour's support, or the dense 007 space. A.8's prose about cones versus equations using spans is itself inconsistent; no project conclusion should rely on treating those as identical.

## What 007 establishes, and the remaining gap

Sources: `docs/reports/007_jsubspace.md`, `docs/design_specs/007_jsubspace.md` §§3–7, A1–A3; `results/007_jsubspace/analysis/{stage1a,brew_test,stage2}.json`; `docs/context/AMENDMENT_02.md`.

Oracle L40 colon: registered P1/P2/P3/P5 and H_J confirm on development and 49 formerly untouched test tables. Descriptive D_z is approximately 5.76 from J versus 3.22 whole on development, 5.03 versus 2.87 on test, with residual 0.30/0.29. These differences survived the intended positive-control/contrast rules. P3/P5 are especially useful because some low-D random/PCA pieces failed their own sensitivity checks; one must not read every raw contrast as equally diagnostic. A1 permits a failed positive-control piece's clear positive D to stand, while treating its null as uninformative.

This is a reader sensitivity result: the Oracle consumes `16000 * unit(piece)` (§3.2), so projected and whole activations have equal input norm to the Oracle. D is a double difference of colour log likelihoods, not spontaneous truthful reporting. D need not be additive over components. Greater D from J alone can reflect removal of interfering information and changed normalized direction, not more intermediate information in an information-theoretic sense.

The model-side edit is instead `h_m + P_J(h_p-h_m)`: it replaces a counterfactual component while keeping the rest of the natural member activation. Sharing P_J does not make this the same experimental manipulation as reading `unit(P_J h)` in an auxiliary network. The endpoints also differ (reader colour likelihood versus subject answer margin), so their nats or relative amplification cannot be directly compared as a common causal scale.

Stage 2 primary rule (§5.3): recovery R=S/S_full, restricted to S_full>=0.10; both J−PCA4 and J−mean(random-delta) must be >=0.10 with positive 95% CIs for “J beyond content share.” “Tracks delta-share” requires **both 90% CIs inside ±0.10**. Otherwise mixed, unless both meet the negative rule. Thus not passing the privilege margin is not equivalent to passing the equivalence rule.

## Two specific interpretation caveats requiring attention

### Complementary clamp fixes joint coefficients, not isolated J scores

Small code path: `scripts/008_trackb/trackb.py` `B_make`, approximately lines 409–430; `Builder.mats`, lines 297–328. For a residual swap the columns are concatenated as V=[n_z,n_z′,Y], where Y contains the union of sparse supports and colour spellings. Coordinates are c=V†(h−mu), and only the first two coordinates are swapped. Remaining **joint-dictionary coefficients** are set to their clean values.

This differs from clamping `Y†(h−mu)` or the lens scores `Yᵀh`. A residual edit δn generally changes Yᵀh unless Yᵀδn=0. Passing RA=I and the saved G-clamp test verifies the implemented joint coordinates, not this stronger condition. A residual for one colour is orthogonal to its own active support; it is not generally orthogonal to the partner's support.

CPU verification from `outputs/008_trackb/directions.pt`, all 82 pairs × 3 sites × 9 layers, separately by lens:

- |cos(J_z,n_z)|: median about 5e−10, maximum below 4e−9.
- |cos(J_z,n_z′)|: median 0.08720 NP / 0.05057 CB; maxima 0.18571 / 0.18080.
- The swap edit has direction n_z′−n_z. Its maximum absolute cosine with the four included lower/capitalized colour atoms has median 0.02551 NP / 0.02374 CB and maximum 0.07495 / 0.07133. These are direction-level diagnostics, not measured post-write score changes.

Consequently, do not say the clamp prevents all J re-entry or establishes absence of J mediation. Its causal comparison concerns the particular joint-coordinate intervention, at three positions and nine layers. Whether the paper implemented the same joint-coefficient convention is unresolved; this is **not an established implementation bug**. A direct mathematical/CPU audit of independent J-coordinate changes, using saved activations and matrices, is the cheapest next check before making a stronger clamp claim. If the intended claim was to preserve isolated lens coordinates, Claude should consider a constrained edit whose read/write maps enforce those constraints explicitly; that would define a changed experiment, not silently repair the old one.

### Norm matching refers to clean-reference edits

`trackb.py` lines 433–445 computes the scale by comparing projected swap edits evaluated on clean h. Subsequent layer writes set the scaled target from the perturbed h. Their actual norms need not match after prior layers have changed the trajectory.

Saved per-pair `edit_norm_sum_base` confirms this. Ratio of logged cumulative requested raw norm to `B_raw_full`, median [min,max]:

| Nominal raw-norm arm | NP | CB |
|---|---|---|
| J | 1.64731 [1.03316,2.72624] | 1.83268 [1.13761,3.61158] |
| residual | 1.00716 [0.98842,1.02985] | 1.02061 [0.97723,1.06286] |

Therefore “equal raw edit norm” is inaccurate without “clean-reference.” This difference does not by itself explain the weaker J result as a lower delivered dose: J received more cumulative raw perturbation. However, nonmonotonic dose-response effects remain possible, so this is not a definitive efficiency comparison. It limits efficiency/potency statements and a literal matched-dose comparison. The logged sum is a diagnostic, not a complete causal dose metric; per-layer and whitened norms would be needed for a stronger dose comparison.

## Specificity, uncertainty, and alternative explanations

The class means exclude the recipient table, verified in `trackb.py:phase2`, with all 231 development renders used. This is a meaningful protection against table-specific answer memorization. The model-side construction uses true benchmark z labels rather than reader labels. However, only 48 source tables and a limited set of rendered starts provide those means; start/answer frequencies can still correlate with z. General transfer to the target answer is evidence of useful information in the constructed direction, not a unique isolation of the intermediate computation. A finite-sample role-contamination census and relationships between intervention outcomes and target-answer versus z-coordinate alignment could be checked from saved materials before new GPU work.

Bootstrap code correctly resamples tables and retains all pairs of a sampled table (`scripts/common/stats.py:cluster_bootstrap`, called by `trackb_analysis.py`). The point estimate is a median over **pairs**, not equal-weighted table medians. This is appropriate if declared, but tables contributing more pairs carry more point-estimate weight. Thirty-three clusters and shared leave-table-out direction estimates limit the scope of uncertainty: intervals condition on the trained direction construction; they do not include uncertainty from retraining the means or covariance. The frozen one-pair-per-table subset is useful sensitivity evidence, but its whitened full arm fails the power gate (S=0.090577…). It cannot independently confirm the whitened result.

Damage is incompletely summarized in the canonical report. Recomputed from existing rows: full raw has both answer endpoints reduced by >0.05 nats in 3/82 cases; median target-answer log-probability rises 1.29961 nats and correct-answer log-probability falls 0.34141. Thus the effect is not merely suppressing both endpoints. The matched random direction conditions are much weaker, a useful specificity control.

However, `off_pair_mass_edit - off_pair_mass_clean > .05` occurs in 58/82 full-raw, 58/82 NP-residual, and 57/82 CB-residual cases, versus 4/82 NP-J and 15/82 CB-J. This quantity is probability outside the two answer first-token sets (`regime.py:sec_damage`), **not outside all valid colours**. It cannot itself establish generic damage. The endpoint shifts and donor-specific margin must be presented alongside it. `top1_retention` median is 1, but it measures only a few teacher-forced prompt positions after the first edited position, not a general capability task. The stored `flip` is top colour among the twelve accepted colours, not necessarily the full-vocabulary top token; label it accordingly when comparing the paper's top-one outcomes.

## Highest-value existing-evidence questions

1. At the *same on-contract layer and common set of pairs*, how tightly is Oracle dense-J dependence associated with subject-model dense-J influence? Aggregate different-instrument contrasts do not answer this. Existing 007 per-pair reader and patch outputs allow the descriptive association, with table clustering and explicit normalization; a null would require adequate spread and sensitivity.
2. Does the 008 complementary clamp preserve the claimed J measurement? Compute isolated Y†h and/or Yᵀh before/after the exact saved-matrix intervention and distinguish joint coefficients, lens scores, and transformed activations. This settles the immediate semantic ambiguity without another model run.
3. How target-specific is the powered full/residual intervention? Audit saved endpoint shifts, off-pair mass, dependence on class imbalance and role correlations. If archived outputs do not contain full edited colour probabilities, say so; do not reconstruct them from S. A future experiment should test the same intermediate direction across changed downstream mappings, or controls that separate answer and intermediate directions, before asserting natural intermediate use.

The most decisive existing result for the local privilege question is the primary natural-coordinate 008 contrast: both dictionaries give much weaker sparse-J effects while the full and remainder interventions have clear leverage and the matched random controls are weak. Its decisive scope is the constructed intervention family. The least convincing remaining interpretation is a strong, same-layer reader–model dissociation phrased as “Oracle privileges J, model does not”: the dense model result is mixed at the Oracle's layers, and sparse/dense plus read/write manipulations still must not be conflated.
