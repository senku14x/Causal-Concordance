## Question and measurement conventions

North Star: do readers recover content the subject demonstrably uses, assessed independently of readers? Main unmet assumption: a stable z-specific causal reference. Whole-cell effects measure combined pair differences, not automatically z use. Agreement ≠ truth; decodability ≠ use; steering ≠ natural route.

Brew: same colour table, changed start; hidden intermediate z, final colour a. Read cells: assistant newline, **newline after `</think>`** (shorthand `</think>`), colon after Answer. S=delta_m/G; m is full-sequence LSE logP(partner accepted spellings) minus clean answer; G is natural counterfactual gap. S ≠ accuracy or causal fraction. D=[LL_m(t_m)−LL_m(t_p)]−[LL_p(t_m)−LL_p(t_p)], partners averaged per table. Common probability scaling cancels; temperature does not. Bootstrap usually10k table clusters, seed20261004. Pair point estimands still weight tables with more pairs more heavily. Use full precision, half-up rounding.
