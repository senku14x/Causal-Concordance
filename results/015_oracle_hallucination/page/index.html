Oracle hallucination test, with cross-scoring

013 found that the NLA's false claims help its own reconstructor rebuild the activation, through misquotes of the prompt. 015 ran the same pipeline on the Oracle Lens and scored every version of both readers' texts with both readers' reconstructors. Under the Oracle's reconstructor, the true answer carries the most signal, and more than the same tokens cut from another bullet's opening. The Oracle's false claims also carry signal, and they are mostly wrong answers. Under the NLA's reconstructor, none of this moves. No difference between the readers survives the pre-registered rule: each reconstructor rewards its own reader's kind of text.

What was tested

Two readers wrote text about the same final-token activations: the Oracle Lens (bullets that continue the prompt, read at L44) and the NLA (prose descriptions, read at L42). That is 87 multihop items × three states (clean, J-lens swap, random swap) × 2 samples = 522 texts each. Each reader has a co-trained reconstructor (AR) that maps text back to an activation. The test deletes or rewrites parts of a text, then asks how much worse the reconstruction gets compared with a matched control. Each text is scored by both ARs, so a result can be pinned to the reader's text or to the reconstructor.

The answer arm (the main contrast)

Under the Oracle AR, deleting the Oracle's answer statements costs Δ_A +14.5 [+11.3, +17.9] points more than deleting the same number of tokens as random clauses. On the 202 readouts where a position-matched control exists (the same token count cut from the opening of a bullet with no answer), the answer still costs Δ_AP +4.3 [+2.0, +6.7]. Δ_A on those same readouts is +5.5 [+3.5, +7.5], so the opening position accounts for about a fifth of the effect there. Under the NLA AR, the Oracle's answers don't matter (0.0 [−2.0, +1.9]). The NLA's own answer statements carry a little under its AR (+2.7 [+1.9, +3.7]), which is a lot per token: they are 5.2% of its text.

False claims vs random text (primary Δ) and span kinds

The pre-registered expectation was that the Oracle's false claims would be padding for its reconstructor. They are not: the primary Δ is +9.0 [+4.8, +13.2]. By kind, the signal is in wrong answers (wrong_entity +10.4 [+5.4, +15.9]; the judge's "misquote" tags on Oracle text are also wrong answers, every one in the hand checks, +10.9 [+5.5, +16.9]). Invented context is padding (−2.6 [−4.3, −0.8]). Wrong answers sit in the same slot as the true answer: the first words of a bullet, the text that follows the read position. On NLA texts, 013's result reproduces exactly (+28.6 [+24.0, +33.4], carried by misquotes +35.8 [+31.1, +40.8]).

Kinds use the clause control (Rk − C1k). Hover a dot for its n; every n is in the report's tables.

Rewrites

013's factual rewrite F is mostly deletion for the Oracle: it removes follow-on questions and placeholders. Both reconstructors use that text, so F − P is −14.6 [−17.8, −11.4] under the Oracle AR and −21.5 [−25.6, −17.2] under the NLA AR, against +3.1 [+1.3, +4.9] for the NLA under its own AR. A correction-only rewrite (Fc, post hoc: fix each flagged span in place, delete nothing) does not help either reconstructor. Fc − Pc is −1.5 [−2.5, −0.6] under the Oracle AR and −0.9 [−2.2, +0.2] under the NLA AR, and it is negative in clean states as well (post hoc check). CW-20 also found that correcting the wrong pieces changes nothing.

Caveats

  • The reconstructors are each reader's co-trained critic. This measures what each critic rewards in text, not what Qwen computes.
  • Scope: WorkspaceBench multihop through Jan Chen's bank, the final prompt token, Oracle L44 and NLA L42, Gemini 3.8 Flash and GLM-5.3-flash judges, 2 samples per state.
  • Judge recall misses leave some answer text in CA, so Δ_A is if anything understated. The Oracle's "misquote" kind is unreliable: its tags were wrong in 6 of 6 hand checks.
  • The position-matched control exists only for readouts with an answer-free bullet (202 of 408), so Δ_AP comes from readouts with more wrong-answer bullets.
  • The Fc arm, the scale-free view and the per-state Fc check are post hoc and descriptive. Everything is exploratory.
  • Paraphrase matching is imperfect (Oracle P within 20% of F's edit size on 308 of 522); the matched subset gives the same signs.

Choices fixed now (the spec leaves them open, or they fix a defect; POST HOC where marked). 1. Delimiter strip (POST HOC, fixes a defect): one leading <<< and one trailing >>> are stripped from every P and P* before scoring. words_changed and the match ratio are recomputed on the stripped text. The re-ask is not redone. P's B labels were judged on the wrapped text and are kept. 2. PA construction: 5 seeds, as for R1 and RA.

  • Answer spans are placed longest first. Each goes to a seeded random answer-free host (an Oracle bullet's content after - , or an NLA sentence) with at least as many tokens. The first l tokens are cut at the tokenizer's offsets.
  • If any seed runs out of hosts, PA is not computable for that text.
  • Smoke coverage: Oracle 10/20, NLA 12/20. An Oracle readout whose bullets all open with the answer has no host, so Δ_AP is measured on readouts with more wrong or placeholder bullets.

3. RA: built as R1 (clause pool = all clause units). On Oracle texts, a bullet's first clause unit includes the - mark, which the Oracle AR does not score. So RA can delete about one scored token fewer than CA per bullet-initial clause it takes. 4. New kinds: wrong_entity and false_fact use 013's construction with new salts (clause 61 / 71, sentence 81 / 91). 013's kinds keep 013's salts. The rebuilt NLA versions equal 013's stored texts for 20/20 smoke explanations. 5. Emptied bullets: a line with no word character after deletion (e.g. a lone -) is dropped from the Oracle AR's average. A version with no piece left is degenerate and counted. 6. J share under the Oracle AR: 013's form on unit-normalised raw output, centred at the mean of the unit-normalised raw L44 gold over the 261 targets (direction only, so the AR's per-layer scale cancels). 7. Per-share version: a ratio of means, Σ Δ / Σ deleted token share, with an item-cluster bootstrap. A per-text ratio is unstable for small deletions. For the reader contrast it is computed per reader, then differenced. 8. English-only subset: the (item, state, sample) whose Oracle readout has no non-Latin script.
