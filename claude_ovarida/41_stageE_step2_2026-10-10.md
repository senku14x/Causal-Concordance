## Readings

Rungs as the collaborator guide uses them.
- **Observation (brew).** Phrase vectors align with the model's class-mean colour difference at `:` about half as well
  as single words (0.077 vs 0.149 at L40), and decode worse.
- **Interpretation (brew).** Brew's reference is the model's "about to say this colour" direction (brew is
  output-facing; 006b, 009). The AR's target is the state *before* its input. So a bare " red" or "the red" gives
  close to that state, while topic-diverse phrases ("Red velvet cake…", "Red flags in your financial statement…") add
  content that dilutes it.
  - So better phrases do not fix 011's "half as good"; the brief's question "was 011's gap noise?" gets a no at the
    vector level.
  - This is not tested by an edit, and the gate's specificity measure itself rewards answer-facing vectors on brew.
- **Observation (multihop).** Phrases, single words and templates align about equally with the model's own concept
  differences (about 0.33–0.35 at L40). "Mentions" alone is the weakest and least stable set.
- **Observation (J).** The AR's concept differences are more J-concentrated than the model's class-mean differences on
  both families (multihop L40: 0.59 vs 0.43). This is consistent with 011's AR J-bias check (1.27× at L40), on new
  material.

## Changes and mistakes during step 2

These are recorded in 012E §14 and the `CLAUDE.md` log. None changes a threshold or a rule.

**Changes made during the run:**
1. **Generator `max_tokens` 2000 → 16000.** The smoke run's responses were truncated: about 1,920 reasoning tokens at
   effort "minimal".
2. **Cue-varied prompts: 3 requests × 24 per concept.** In the France smoke, 7 of 21 prompts passed the gate.
3. **A fallback parser** for one response whose phrases were unquoted (brew "green", "starts").
4. **Answer matcher v2.** v1 kept only ASCII letters and prefix-matched inside words. v2 changes 42 concepts' gate
   rows; those were re-gated, and the v1 records are kept.

**Bugs:**

5. **A launcher bug of mine** left GPU 1 idle for about 3 minutes.
6. **Found while writing this report:** the split-half seed used Python's `hash()` of the concept name, which is
   randomised per process. So the stabilities committed in `e0b3ee0` could not be reproduced: brew phrases read 0.68
   there and 0.66 in the committed JSON, and are 0.70 now.
   - Fixed with a stable CRC32 seed. Two reruns now give identical values, and every verdict is unchanged.
   - Only the stabilities depend on the split. Specificity, decodability and J share do not.
   - A single split is still noisy for brew's 12 colours: brew "mentions" read 0.39–0.50 across the three splits seen.

## Decisions for the PI

1. **Brew.** Accept the stop? Recommended. A post hoc brew run with single-word or template vectors would mostly
   repeat 011; its only new piece is the J-removed arm (about 15 GPU-minutes).
2. **The multihop primary AR vector.**
   - Pooled phrases is recommended: it is stable, its decodability is best, and it matches single words on alignment.
   - Single words are the alternative.
   - "Mentions" alone fails stability (0.76–0.78), so it stays secondary.
3. **Class-mean coverage (38/82 items).** Either accept it, with fix 2's J-row-size fallback for the other items, or
   regenerate easier, well-known-fact prompts for the 43 dropped concepts. The second is post hoc, about $1 and about
   30 GPU-minutes.
4. **Go for step 4:** the multihop parity check at L36–47 plus a 5-item smoke, with items split across both GPUs.
