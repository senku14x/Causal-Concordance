## (a) The paper in my own words

**What it builds.** For each layer ℓ, the Jacobian lens takes the Jacobian of the (penultimate- or final-layer)
residual stream at every later position t′ ≥ t with respect to the layer-ℓ residual at t, and averages it over
positions and about 1,000 pretraining-like prompts: one d×d matrix J_ℓ per layer. The readout is
softmax(W_U · norm(J_ℓ h)). The rows of W_U J_ℓ are one direction per vocabulary token: the average linearised
disposition of a direction to make the model say that token, now or later. Averaging is the point: it is meant to
separate "poised to be said" from "used in this context". The **J-space** is the set of sparse (k ≲ 25) non-negative
combinations of these vectors: a union of cones, not a subspace. Its "component" in an activation is found by
non-negative gradient pursuit. (A.8's formula uses linear spans and orthogonal projection, which describes a larger set
than the cones in the prose: the inconsistency the PI's notes flag.)

**How it intervenes.** Steering (h + αv), ablation (remove the projection on v), and the **coordinate swap**:
V = [v_s v_t], c = V⁺h, h′ = h + V(σ(c) − c). It changes only the component in span{v_s, v_t}. (012 showed this is a
reflection of the src−tgt contrast, so its direction depends on the clean coordinates.) "Clamped" swaps set the
coordinates at every position over a layer band.

**Main claims (Claude Sonnet 4.5 by default; Haiku/Opus 4.5 and Opus 4.6 for some).**
1. *Functional:* J-space content supports **verbal report** (swaps move the reported item into the top-5 on 88% of
   trials); responds to **instructions** (focus / ignore, though mention alone primes almost as much); carries
   **intermediates of silent reasoning** (two-hop swaps flip the answer on 54/70/70% for Haiku/Sonnet/Opus; the
   intermediate swap acts about 17% of depth earlier than the answer swap; arithmetic intermediates appear in compute
   order, confirmed by lens-free mean-difference patching); is a valid argument to **many downstream functions**
   (76/192, 101/192 at α = 2; countries 42/48, number words 0/48); and is **selective**: needed for report and
   flexible inference, not for continuation, anomaly detection or line wrapping (n = 8 and 11 passages), with
   whole-J-space ablation hurting recall and generation tasks more than classification.
2. *Structural:* workspace-like only in a middle band (~L38–92 on the 0–100 scale); about 25 active vectors and < 10%
   excess variance over a random dictionary; preferentially amplified by MLPs (~10×) and moved by a small set of
   "broadcast" heads, whose ablation cuts injected-thought report 0.54 → 0.09.
3. *Applications:* auditing readouts (blackmail, prompt injection, Opus 4.6 cases), model organisms, base vs
   post-trained, counterfactual reflection training (fabrication 0.25 → 0.07; ablating implanted tokens → 0.22).

**The privilege test (§3.1 Fig. 8, §3.3 Fig. 16): the result this project leans on most.** Take a concept or
intermediate vector built without the lens ("Tell me about X" means; or a cue-varied mean-difference probe), split it
by pursuit into a J component (k = 16 or 25; 6–7% / 10–15% of its variance) and a remainder, and swap each part at
matched norm. Report: J lens vectors 88%, J component 59%, remainder 5% (prose values; the Fig. 8 bars read about 87 / 55 / 9;
corrected 2026-10-10). Two-hop (n = 90): raw J 60%, J component
61%, remainder 28%, which falls to 6% when the J coordinates are clamped to clean values at every position and layer.
So the J component carries most of the causal effect at a fraction of the variance, and the remainder's effect runs
back through J.

**Verbalizers (A.9).** The **template lens** (A.9.1): a whitened class-mean (LDA) direction per word, from generated
contexts that precede it; reads multi-token words (blackmail, photosynthesis) and swaps Tchaikovsky↔Beethoven; but it
skips to the answer like the tuned lens. The **oracle lens** (A.9.2): a frozen phrase→activation reconstructor builds
a 3.4M-phrase dictionary; non-negative OMP labels train an oracle (an activation injected into a copy of the model)
to emit K phrases of length N; RL then rewards whitened FVE. 31% FVE on held-out assistant activations, illustrative
examples only. The authors argue it confabulates less than NLAs because its reconstructor is frozen and linear in
templates, not co-trained. Our "Oracle Lens" is WorkspaceBench's reproduction of this (AR + inverter + RL on FVE),
not a Karvonen oracle.

**What the paper does *not* show.**
- Anything about phenomenal consciousness, or the full GWT architecture (no encapsulated modules, no recurrence).
- That all strategic or misaligned cognition routes through J (automatic behaviour may bypass it), or that J-space
  monitoring is sufficient. No false-positive rates for any auditing readout.
- That early layers are empty: it may be a lens artifact (the authors say so; R-lens supports it partly).
- An independent test of "broadcast": the directions are selected by a Jacobian through the very MLPs and heads whose
  gain is then measured (selection bias, as 04b puts it).
- A predictive criterion for which tasks need J; the oracle lens has no causal test and no baselines on its FVE.
- Anything beyond Claude models: Nanda's and Zeisler's Qwen replications are partial (swap flips 6–11%, answer swaps
  dominate; poetry and arithmetic failed).
- Many choices (k ∈ {10, 16, 25}, layer bands, curated token lists, selected examples) have no sensitivity analysis or
  pre-registration.

## (b) The PI's notes

**What the PI stresses (04, 04b).**
- The well-supported core is narrow: a token-aligned format exists that carries causally load-bearing intermediates.
  The strongest pieces are the privilege splits with clamps (twice), the 17%-earlier timing, the bandit and rhyme
  swaps, lens-free arithmetic patching, the line-wrap dissociation and A.12's probe dissociation.
- The structural claims are fragile. The band is lens-derived (ignition and the composition bump are the only
  lens-free corroboration), and broadcast is partly selected for.
- **Report ≠ behaviour.** The eval-awareness ablation removes both the "this is a test" text and the latent
  recognition, then samples. 0/180 → 13/180 is small and may be prompt-concentrated.
- Other confounds: experiential ablation removes the vocabulary the judge rewards; the model-organism SDF signal could
  be lexical exposure; the ablation battery confounds "flexible vs automatic" with output format.
- **Scope and researcher degrees of freedom:** Claude-only, small n, curated lists, no pre-registration.
- Follow-ups that set expectations for Qwen:
  - Ratnaditya: a released lens as a detector doesn't beat TF-IDF by the pre-registered margin;
  - R-lens: earlier legibility, with Biderman's "K+2" objection;
  - WorkspaceBench: Oracle-RL is the best reader on most families. But the prompt-only baseline is often as strong,
    and the NLA's hallucination rate (≈ 0.96) is a readout-level "≥ 1 contradicted claim" rate, not a per-claim
    rate (04b).
- §11's catalogue of measures and §12's open questions. Questions 3 (report vs behaviour), 4 (false positives), 5
  (output format) and 8 (oracle vs NLA, prompt-only bar) are the ones closest to this project.

**Where 04b refines 04.**
- Drop Nanda's "no time for nonlinear processing" intuition: derivatives propagate through every downstream
  nonlinearity.
- "Broadcast is tautological" → "selection bias toward output-sensitive directions": next-MLP gain and
  label-preserving heads are not guaranteed by definition.
- The tuned lens doesn't "ignore the input": its advantage comes mostly from the bias.
- The logit lens's reward/bias counts are small, not zero.
- Counterfactual reflection training ablates the top 10 of the candidate tokens locally, not all 176 / 63
  everywhere. On deception the trained-vs-base advantage drops only 0.33 → 0.25 under matched ablation, not "the
  full 0.18".
- Fig. 8's prose percentages differ from its bars. "Nearly the entire 80-word family" is stronger than the plot. 16 × 40
  carriers in the text vs 49 in an inset. Final vs penultimate target layer is ambiguous.

**Where 03 §14 corrects the PI's transcriptions.**
- Ratnaditya's control AUC is 0.591 with a CI that excludes 0.5. "At chance" and "does not hallucinate" overstate it.
- WorkspaceBench prompt-only values were assigned to the wrong families. 0.96 belongs to brew, not buggy code (≈ .04);
  moral rationale ≈ .49; .82 is relational; multihop prompt-only ≈ .90 beats J-lens .82; poetry ≈ .71 beats
  Oracle-RL .55.
- J-lens brew .86 is the best reader but below prompt-only. NLA multihop-mt is ≈ .22; .75 is plain multihop.
- Hallucination: logit ≈ .20, tuned ≈ .27, R ≈ .21; Patchscopes lowest ≈ .06.
- Zeisler's Qwen run on Anthropic's own items still has answer swaps dominating, so "too easy" isn't supported.

**Where 03 §15 disagrees with the PI (open).**
1. Report privilege (§3.1) is partly by construction: J vectors are chosen for output effect, so an equal-norm
   comparison favours them. §3.3's probe split is the informative one.
2. The reflection-training base-model control is weak: the ablated tokens are those that increased, so ablation
   removes little norm in the base model. It needs a norm-matched ablation in the trained model and an SFT control.
3. The ablation's impairment is causal; "automatic vs flexible" stays a hypothesis.
4. Halsall's VJP coherence may reflect a shared generic direction (frequency offset), not concept content.
5. R-lens is not yet direct evidence of early workspace content (K+2; token-identity damage).

My own read, for the PI to check: the project's results so far sit on 03 §15.1's side. On Qwen, the report-like
measures (005) turned out to be priming, and the model-side privilege test did not reproduce (008).
