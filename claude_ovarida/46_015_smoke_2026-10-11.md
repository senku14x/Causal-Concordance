## 2. What the smoke shows (descriptive; n 20)

### 2a. The answer arm (the main contrast)

- **Oracle texts under the Oracle AR:**
  - Deleting the answer spans (14.6% of tokens) lowers FVE by +24.6 [+12.4, +37.0] (O − CA). Token-matched random
    clause deletion costs less, so Δ_A = **+15.9 [+7.0, +24.1]**. Per unit of deleted share: +108.4 [+59.6, +155.8].
  - **The position-matched control eats most of that gap:** Δ_AP = +5.3 [−0.7, +12.1] (n 10).
    - PA cuts the same number of tokens from the opening of an answer-free bullet. In these readouts that bullet usually
      opens with a wrong answer or a placeholder ("Stockholm…", "Pt", "[Answer]").
    - **Reading (hypothesis):** much of what the Oracle AR rewards is the opening of a bullet, true answer or not, as
      CW-20 could not rule out. The full run decides it.
  - **Mechanistic hypothesis, not tested:** the Oracle AR predicts the state *before* a span. A bullet that opens with an
    answer is next-token text for the last prompt token. Cutting a bullet's first tokens turns it into a mid-sentence
    fragment (", and its currency is…"), whose preceding state is a different kind of state.
- **CW-20 consistency (her metric):** O 0.213 (hers 0.21); RA − CA +0.046, against her +0.077 in our sign convention.
  Same direction, somewhat smaller, different samples.
- **Under the NLA AR,** Oracle texts give Δ_A +3.9 [−5.8, +13.7] and Δ_AP −1.2 [−5.2, +2.9], both unresolved.
- **NLA texts under the NLA AR:** Δ_A +4.7 [+1.2, +9.6] (n 13). The NLA's answer statements are only 5.4% of its
  tokens, so per unit of share this is large (+86.0 [+23.6, +188.6]).

### 2b. The primary false-claim Δ

- **Oracle texts:** +1.1 [−6.2, +8.5] (Oracle AR) and +3.8 [−1.9, +10.0] (NLA AR), n 14, both unresolved.
  - A flags 14.0% of the Oracle's tokens (the NLA's: 61.3%), so each deletion is small next to per-text noise.
  - The pre-stated expectation ("padding or no difference") can't be judged at this n.
- **NLA texts under the NLA AR:** +29.1 [+9.1, +54.1].
  - The misquote kind is +48.7 [+25.5, +75.3] and invented context −9.3 [−18.5, −2.4]. That is 013's pattern, on 20 of
    013's explanations.
- **Per-kind on Oracle texts:** n is 2–9 per kind, and misquote is mis-tagged (§1), so nothing is read.

### 2c. The rewrite arm on the Oracle is mostly deletion

- **Oracle texts:**
  - Under the Oracle AR: F − P = −7.6 [−12.4, −3.5]; with P within 20% of F's edit size, −11.0 [−16.1, −7.0] (n 10).
    F − O −13.7 and P − O −6.1, both CIs below 0.
  - Under the NLA AR: F − P −14.1 [−33.5, +1.1].
- **NLA texts under the NLA AR:** F − P = +7.8 [+3.7, +11.9]; F − P* = +7.7 [+3.8, +11.9]. The P redo changes nothing.
- **What F removes:**
  - Both readers' F remove about the same share of words: 0.30 of O's words net for each.
  - Outright deleted runs are 0.15 (Oracle) and 0.13 (NLA). The rest comes from replaced runs that shrink: for the
    Oracle, 305 → 94 words.
  - But A flags only 14% of the Oracle's tokens. The hand check shows F mainly deleting follow-on questions,
    placeholders and invented hints, which A did not flag. These "invented context" deletions follow 013's PROMPT_F.
- **Reading (hypothesis):**
  - The Oracle's follow-on questions restate the topic ("What is the capital of Japan?"), and both reconstructors use
    them.
  - For the NLA, F deleted invented context that its AR treats as padding (−9.3 above), and corrected misquotes.
  - So **"F − P" measures different edits for the two readers.** For the Oracle it is not a correction effect.
- **Paraphrase sensitivity:**
  - Paraphrasing alone costs the Oracle AR 6.1 points (P − O −6.1 [−10.1, −2.3]), and the NLA AR only 0.8 on NLA texts.
  - Points don't compare across scorers, but within the Oracle AR, wording changes the size of an F − P effect.

None meets the claim rule, as expected at 13 (item, state) pairs.

- **Δ_A:** −18.3 [−26.7, −9.2] under the Oracle AR, +3.3 [−6.2, +12.2] under the NLA AR. By §7's rule, a contrast

- **English-only:** 4 of the 20 Oracle readouts have no non-Latin script (this sample is 80% CJK or full-width

## 3. Problems found

**3a. P delimiters (fixed before scoring).**
- Gemini returned 8/20 Oracle P texts as `<<<…>>>`, echoing PROMPT_P's delimiters around the text it was given. They
  are stripped, with words_changed recomputed.
- The re-ask is not redone. The B labels of P were judged on the wrapped text and are kept.
- **013's P has the same defect on 4/522 texts:** lisbon J 0, tajmahal rand 1, olympia J 0, caesar rand 1. 013's
  recorded F − P (+3.1) includes them. It is not changed here.

**3b. Kind tags on Oracle readouts.** Judge B tags a wrong answer that opens a bullet as "misquote" (3/3 in the
smoke). Its PROMPT_K order puts misquote first, and "versions that swap in an entity or answer" invites it. The Oracle
per-kind misquote numbers are reported as unreliable. wrong_entity, which CW-20's "wrong pieces" mostly were, is
undercounted to the same degree.

**3c. 013's random controls sometimes delete everything** (found while checking 14 degenerate entries in this smoke).
- With C1 kept just above the 10-token floor, the matcher's "one more unit if closer" step can take every unit.
- **In 013's eligible set, R1 is empty for at least one seed on 12/474 explanations (26 texts), RS1 on 75/474
  (208 texts).**
- The NLA AR scores an empty text through its template at FVE −142 to −193 on the 5 smoke cases. C1 for the same
  explanations scores −64 to −158.
- **Direction:** this pulls R1 and RS1 down, so it biases 013's primary and sentence-control Δ toward 0 (conservative
  for its H-signal). For the invented-context padding reading it biases toward padding, but only on 2/354 (Ri) and
  6/354 (RSi) explanations.
- 013's numbers are not changed. Under the Oracle AR, empty texts are degenerate and excluded (14 entries here).

**3d. PA coverage and selection.**
- PA is computable on 10/20 Oracle and 12/20 NLA texts. An Oracle readout whose bullets all open with the answer has
  no answer-free host.
- **Δ_AP is therefore measured on readouts with more wrong or placeholder bullets**, a different subset from Δ_A's 19.
- Δ_A restricted to the PA subset will be reported next to Δ_AP in the full run, so the two are comparable.

**3e. Small things.**
- Tallest's F inserts prompt text.
- 5 over-long answer spans, which delete a little more than the answer.
- RA's clause pool includes each bullet's `- ` mark, which the Oracle AR does not score, so RA can remove about one
  scored token fewer than CA per bullet-initial clause.

## 4. Decisions for the PI before the full run

**4a. The reader contrast under the Oracle AR is near-degenerate on the NLA side.**
- Every version of the NLA texts lands between −171.0 and −164.1 points under the Oracle AR (cosine about 0.05–0.07;
  step 2's R1 for that cell was +9.7). So the paired NLA − Oracle difference under that scorer is roughly minus the
  Oracle's own Δ.
- §7's "same sign under the other scorer" condition makes the NLA AR the binding scorer for any reader-specific claim.
  The rule still works, but a contrast "holding under the Oracle AR" says little.
- **Recommendation:** keep the pre-registered rule unchanged. Also report, descriptively, each cell's Δ divided by
  that cell's step-2 matched − shuffled range (POST HOC; a scale-free view).

**4b. The Oracle's F.** As specified, F mostly deletes unflagged follow-on text (§2c).
- **Option 1 (recommended):** keep F as pre-registered, and add a POST HOC correction-only arm Fc. Fc corrects
  contradicted spans (wrong answers, false facts) in place, deletes nothing else, and is paired with its own matched
  P. It costs about one judge call per Oracle readout plus a P, about $0.5, and separates "correcting false claims"
  from "deleting follow-ons".
- **Option 2:** keep F only, with this caveat stated.

**4c. PA coverage.** About half of the Oracle readouts. Recommendation: keep the pre-registered construction, report
Δ_A on the PA subset beside Δ_AP (3d), and do not widen the hosts (widening would be a post hoc change to the main
contrast).

**4d. Oracle per-kind misquote.** Report as unreliable (3b). Alternatively, re-run K with a reader-specific note ("a
wrong answer opening a bullet is wrong_entity, not misquote") as a POST HOC kind pass, about $0.3. Recommendation:
report as unreliable and spend nothing. The per-kind results are descriptive in any case.

**4e. 013's empty controls (3c).** Recommendation: a one-paragraph note in the 013 ledger, no rerun (the bias runs
against 013's headline).

## 5. Full run: plan and cost (on the PI's go)

- **Judges:** the other 502 Oracle readouts (A, B, K, answers, F, P with re-ask, B labels) and 502 NLA explanations
  (answers, P*). At the smoke's rate that is about $1.3, plus about $0.5 if 4b's Fc is chosen. The $10 cap stands.
- **G2's 10 extra hand checks:** 10 seeded readouts from outside the smoke, checked before the full analysis is opened.
- **GPU:**
  - The smoke took 102 s on the Oracle AR (5,677 pieces) and 178 s on the NLA AR (1,170 unique texts). The full run is
    about 26 times larger.
  - That is about 45 min on the Oracle AR (GPU 0) and about 75 min on the NLA AR, split into two shards: GPU 1, and
    GPU 0 once the Oracle AR is done.
  - About 1 h wall-clock.
- Then the analysis, report 47 and the ledger. Stop.
