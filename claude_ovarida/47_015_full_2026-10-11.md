**Scope.**

  `docs/ledger/015_ledger.md`: the full-run amendment (4a–4e), and two construction fixes made before any full-run score

- Material: all 522 Oracle L44 readouts and the 522 NLA L42 explanations of 013, on the same (item, state, sample):
  87 multihop items × clean / J swap / random swap × 2 samples.
- Every version of both readers' texts is scored by both reconstructors: 47,353 version texts, 30,183 unique.
- Numbers: every number comes from `results/015_oracle_hallucination/analysis/oh_all.json` (and, for one post hoc check,

  appendix is that script's output, verbatim.
- Points are each reconstructor's own FVE points (013's metric). They are never compared across reconstructors.
  Δ = FVE(control) − FVE(cleaned). Brackets are 95% item-cluster bootstraps. Bands are 013's (±2 points).
- What this measures: what each reader's **co-trained reconstructor** rewards in each reader's text. It does not
  measure what Qwen computes (spec §11).

## Bottom line

**1. Under the Oracle's own reconstructor, the true answer carries the most signal, and not only because it opens a
bullet.**
- Deleting the answer statements costs **Δ_A +14.5 [+11.3, +17.9]** points more than token-matched random clause
  deletion (n 408). Per unit of deleted token share that is +120.6, three times the false-claim figure (+39.2).
- **Against the position-matched control** (the same number of tokens cut from the opening of a bullet that holds no
  answer), the answer still costs **Δ_AP +4.3 [+2.0, +6.7]** (n 202).
- On those same 202 readouts Δ_A is +5.5 [+3.5, +7.5]. So cutting another bullet's opening costs about 1.2 points more
  than random clauses, and cutting the true answer costs 5.5.
- English-only: Δ_A +12.7 [+9.0, +16.5].
- **Both pre-stated expectations for the answer arm hold.** CW-20's "the rebuild leans on the answer" replicates on 013's
  items and survives the position control she could not run.

**2. The Oracle's false claims are not padding for its reconstructor. The ones that carry are wrong answers.**
- Primary Δ = **+9.0 [+4.8, +13.2]** (n 395; H-signal), against the pre-stated expectation of "padding or no
  difference". It holds in each state: clean +8.5, J swap +8.0, random swap +10.3.
- By kind:
  - wrong answers carry above-average signal: wrong_entity +10.4 [+5.4, +15.9]; "misquote" +10.9 [+5.5, +16.9], where
    the hand checks found every Oracle misquote tag to be a wrong answer (0/6 correct);
  - invented context is padding: −2.6 [−4.3, −0.8];
  - false world facts are unresolved: −1.1 [−2.6, +0.4].
- **Reading (hypothesis):** what the Oracle AR rewards is answer-slot text, the first words of a bullet, which are
  next-token text for the read position. A wrong answer in that slot still carries more signal than random text. A
  correct answer carries more again.

**3. Under the NLA's reconstructor, none of the Oracle-text deletions matter.**
- On Oracle texts: primary −0.8 [−2.8, +1.3]; Δ_A 0.0 [−2.0, +1.9]; Δ_AP +0.7 [−0.8, +2.1]. All unresolved by band,
  each inside about ±2.8. English-only, the primary and Δ_A lean slightly negative (−3.1 and −3.7, CIs below 0).
- What does matter to the NLA AR is losing the Oracle's follow-on text: F − O is −26.0 [−30.2, −21.6].

**4. On NLA texts, 013 replicates exactly, and the NLA's answer statements also carry signal under its own AR.**
- 013 reproduced: primary +28.6 [+24.0, +33.4], misquotes +35.8, F − P +3.1, all equal to 013's record (G-repro 0.0 on
  6,189 values).
- New: Δ_A +2.7 [+1.9, +3.7] and Δ_AP +2.8 [+1.8, +4.0]. The NLA's answer statements are 5.2% of its tokens, so per
  unit of share the answer counts about as much as the false claims (+52.8 vs +45.9).

**5. No reader-specific claim survives the pre-registered rule (§7). The two pre-named contrasts that differ flip sign
between reconstructors, so by the rule they are attributed to the reconstructor.**
- **Primary Δ, NLA − Oracle:** −6.9 [−10.9, −3.2] under the Oracle AR, +29.5 [+23.8, +35.4] under the NLA AR.
- **Δ_A, NLA − Oracle:** −14.3 [−18.3, −10.6] under the Oracle AR, +2.3 [+0.5, +4.1] under the NLA AR.
- **Δ_AP:** neither CI excludes 0.
- **Plainly: each reconstructor rewards its own reader's characteristic text.** The Oracle AR rewards answer-slot text
  (true and wrong answers); the NLA AR rewards restatements of the prompt (misquotes) and, to a lesser degree, answer
  statements.
- "The Oracle's text relies on the answer and the NLA's on the prompt" is a statement about reader × reconstructor
  pairs, not about the readers. That is the reading CW-20 also proposed, now with the 2 × 2 that tests it.

**6. Correcting the Oracle's false claims in place does not help either reconstructor (POST HOC Fc arm).**
- Fc − Pc: −1.5 [−2.5, −0.6] (Oracle AR) and −0.9 [−2.2, +0.2] (NLA AR). Fc − O: −2.7 (Oracle AR).
- **The small cost is there in clean states too** (post hoc: clean −1.7, J −1.0, random −1.8). So it isn't that "wrong"
  answers mirror a swapped activation.
- CW-20 found the same: correcting in place −0.3 [−0.7, +0.2]. Ours in her metric is −0.011.
- **013's F (the factual rewrite) on Oracle texts is mostly deletion:** 0.37 of the words, net. It costs a lot under both
  ARs: F − P −14.6 and −21.5. That measures deleting follow-ons, not correcting.

## 7. Problems found during the run (all in the ledger, with the fixes dated before the scores they govern)

1. **Answer-span locator** (found in the extra-10 hand check, fixed before any full-run score was written).
   - 013's `locate` maps every repeat of a string to its first occurrence, so repeated answers were deleted once.
   - 121/408 Oracle and 11/293 NLA readouts were affected. PA coverage fell from 273 to 202.
   - The smoke's answer arm had the defect.
   - The same check on contradicted spans changes 9/395 Oracle readouts and 0 NLA readouts. C1 keeps 013's
     construction, as registered.
2. **Delimiter echoes.** Judge A wrapped 165/522 Oracle paraphrases, 81/389 Pc and 10/522 P* in PROMPT_P's `<<<` `>>>`,
   or in a single `<` `>`. All stripped. 013's own P has 7/522.
3. **Spend estimate (my mistake).** I estimated $1.8 from the smoke's recorded spend ($0.05). That figure counted only
   a rerun's new calls. Actual: $6.76, under the cap.
4. **Report 46 §2a (my mistake, corrected before the run).** It compared Δ_A and Δ_AP on different subsets. The full
   run reports Δ_A on the PA subset beside Δ_AP.
5. **Splitter difference vs step 2.** The full run drops lines with no word character (for example a closing `---`).
   That changes 2/522 Oracle O texts, one by 3.85 points. On the other 173 checked, O matches step 2 within batch noise
   (max 0.47 points).
6. **013's random controls can delete a whole explanation.** R1 for 12/474, RS1 for 75/474 explanations. This biases
   013's Δ toward 0. It is noted in the 013 ledger; 013 is unchanged. Under the Oracle AR those entries are empty and
   excluded (676).
7. **Judge throughput.** The chunked judge stalled behind GLM-5.3-flash calls that reasoned for 8k–18k tokens despite
   effort "low". Continuous submission fixed it. The outputs are cached and unaffected.

## 8. What this establishes, and what it does not

- **Supported (scoped):** 015's multihop items, final token, Oracle L44 and NLA L42, these judges and these samples.
  - Under the Oracle Lens's co-trained reconstructor, the Oracle's answer statements carry more reconstruction signal
    than random text and than position-matched bullet openings.
  - Its flagged false claims, mostly wrong answers, carry more than random text.
  - Under the NLA's reconstructor, neither does.
  - On NLA texts, 013's results reproduce exactly.
- **Not established:**
  - **That either reader reads what Qwen uses.** These are co-trained critics (spec §11).
  - **Any reader-specific difference.** The only differences flip with the reconstructor.
  - Why wrong answers carry signal: the answer-slot reading is a hypothesis.
  - Anything about the NLA × Oracle AR cell beyond "weak" (cos 0.068).
- **Rung:** recurring pattern / supported empirical claim about the critics, at most. No mechanism.

## 9. Possible next steps (for the PI's choice)

- **Cheapest discriminating test of the answer-slot hypothesis (CPU + GPU, about 30 min):** swap the answer between
  bullets inside a readout (true ↔ wrong answer positions), or replace the true answer with a same-length wrong answer.
  If the Oracle AR rewards slot content tied to the activation, the true answer in the slot should beat a wrong one by
  about Δ_AP.
- **The ambitious version:** the clean result here is that answer reliance is a property of reader × critic pairs. A
  critic-free score would separate readers: the subject model's own likelihood of the continuation given the readout,
  or the Stage I/J intervention outcomes.
- 4b–4e are done. The 4a scale-free view is descriptive and stays so.
