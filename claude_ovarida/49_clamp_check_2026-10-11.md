## Bottom line

**The mechanism holds by the pre-stated rule.** A per-layer clamp along u′ = cos·u + sin·w accumulates the part of the
edit that persists across layers.

| Arm | L47 ratio to AR_full along u | 1/cos(u′, u) | ratio ÷ (1/cos), median over items | position-median version | within 15%? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AR_rrem | 1.13 | 1.12 | 1.01 | 1.03 | yes |
| AR_emrem | 1.62 | 1.63 | 0.97 | 0.88 | yes |
| AR_Jrem (descriptive) | 0.71 | 1.63 | 0.44 | 0.46 | no |

- **The two removal arms out-push AR_full along u by about 1/cos**, which matches their larger effects in the full and
  §17 runs:
  - median Δm AR_rrem 6.09 and AR_emrem 8.43, against AR_full 5.25;
  - ratios 1.16 and 1.60.
  - So "matched size at the clamp target" was not a matched effective push.
- **The accumulation is visible layer by layer** (the median over items of the read-position ratio). It starts near cos
  after one clamp and climbs:
  - AR_emrem: L36 0.68 → L37 1.03 → L39 1.17 → L41 1.31 → L43 1.51 → L45 1.54 → L47 1.62;
  - AR_rrem: L36 0.89 → L37 1.06 → L39 1.06 → L41 1.08 → L43 1.11 → L45 1.11 → L47 1.13.
- **AR_Jrem does not accumulate.**
  - Its push along u stays about 0.7 of AR_full's from the first band layer on: L36 0.67 → L37 0.68 → L39 0.70 → L41 0.67 → L43 0.67 → L45 0.69 → L47 0.71.
  - Its push along u's J direction is small: median 1.24 at L47, against AR_full's push along u of 9.04.
  - Yet it re-applies more per layer than AR_full. The median realised ‖Δh‖/‖h‖ at the read position at L40 is
    0.0525, against 0.0099; AR_emrem is 0.0507.
  - **Reading (descriptive, 10 items):** the part of u that persists across L36–47, and so accumulates under a clamp,
    lies in J. The non-J part fades, and is re-added each layer without building up.
- **The instrument is exact:** AR_full's own push along u at L47 is 0.997–1.013 of k across the 10 items (the clamp, up to the bf16 write).

## What this changes (dated caveats added today)

- **Report 44's "J-specific" reading.**
  - The pre-stated rule held: AR_emrem − AR_Jrem > 0 and AR_emrem keeps ≥ 0.5 of AR_full. But the two arms were not at
    a matched effective push along u: about 1.6× AR_full's for AR_emrem against about 0.7× for AR_Jrem.
  - Removing J removes both the J content and the part of u that persists across layers.
  - Whether a J-removed direction would act at a matched effective push is untested.
  - The statement stays as "removing J removes the effect at matched clamp size", with this caveat (report 44, 012E
    §18 pointer, ledger).
- **Report 43's "random removal makes the edit stronger"** is now explained by the accumulation, not by renormalisation
  (which was already withdrawn).
- **008's J vs non-J clamps carry the same unquantified caveat** (note added to `docs/reports/008_trackb.md` §5).
- **A CLAUDE.md pitfall is added:** per-layer clamps of mixed directions are not matched in effective push by matching k.

## Gate (pre-stated: realised edit sizes reproduce the recorded runs bitwise)

- **AR_full and AR_emrem:** bitwise equal, at every layer and position. The rebuilt emrem cosines are identical too
  (max |Δcos| 0).
- **AR_rrem and AR_Jrem: failed as stated.** The max absolute difference in ‖Δh‖/‖h‖ is 0.0009 and
  0.0019. The medians are 2.2e-06 and 1.5e-08, against a typical value
  of 0.0040.
  - rrem's random QR on the CPU varies with the thread count (5e-8).
  - Jrem's direction is thread-invariant; the source of its tiny differences was not identified.
  - These are 4 to 6 orders of magnitude below the ratios read here.
